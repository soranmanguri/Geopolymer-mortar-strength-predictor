Binder System

Precursor solids — at least one of fly ash, slag, or metakaolin.

Alkaline Activator

Sodium hydroxide / sodium silicate activation system. The Na₂SiO₃/NaOH ratio is calculated automatically from the two solution masses.

Nano-Silica Modification

Colloidal nano-silica dosage and particle size. Particle size locks to 0 whenever dosage is 0 — size is meaningless with no nano-silica in the mix.

Physical Mix & Curing

Sand is calculated automatically (see Mix Volume Check below) to fill the remaining volume, or enter your own value to override the auto-fill; Alk:Binder is calculated from the activator and binder masses; curing regime is set directly.

Mix Volume Check

Absolute volume method: Binder + Activator solution (NaOH + Na₂SiO₃) + Nano-Silica + Voids occupy part of 1 m³ (1000 L); Sand automatically fills whatever volume remains, unless you enter your own Sand value below to override it.
